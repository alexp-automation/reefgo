## Prohibited to spear (FL, any method of spearing)
Billfish, spotted eagle ray, manta ray, sturgeon, sharks, bonefish, tarpon, goliath grouper, Nassau grouper, snook, spotted seatrout, red drum, weakfish, pompano, African pompano, permit, tripletail, blue crab, stone crab, lobster; ornamental families: surgeonfish, trumpetfish, angelfish, butterflyfish, porcupinefish, cornetfish, squirrelfish, trunkfish, damselfish, parrotfish, pipefish, seahorse, puffers, triggerfish (except gray and ocean).

## Where spearing is prohibited
- Within 100 yd of a public swimming beach, commercial/public fishing pier, or bridge section open to public fishing
- Within 100 ft of the above-water part of a jetty (except last 500 yd of a jetty longer than 1,500 yd)
- Monroe County from Long Key north to the Dade County line
- Freshwater; Florida Park Service waters; Volusia inland waters (except flounder/sheepshead with ≤3-prong spear)
- Powerheads, bangsticks, rebreathers prohibited (rebreathers allowed for lionfish only)
- State Reef Fish Angler designation required for private-vessel reef fish; fish landed head and fins intact; dive flag (state law, separate page)

## Species by reef depth (FL Gulf, for the app's "fish here" card)
- < 25 ft: sheepshead (winter), gray snapper, flounder (sand), Spanish mackerel (spring/fall), black sea bass (north Gulf), hogfish (occasional)
- 25–60 ft: + hogfish, gag, red grouper, lane snapper, gray triggerfish, cobia (spring), goliath (wrecks, protected), barracuda
- > 60 ft: + red snapper, greater amberjack, scamp, vermilion, mutton (south), king mackerel

Find your water

ReefGo covers the whole US coast. Share your location once and the map opens on your stretch of coast with the reefs you can actually reach.

Your location stays on this phone. It is never stored or sent anywhere except to fetch the local forecast.

How to read

Green — yes
Yellow — only if you really want to
Red — no

Two separate answers: Go (can you get there and back) and Fish (can you sit on the reef at anchor: wind, gusts, seas and period) or Dive (can you safely be in the water: seas, period and clarity). Computed for the time window you pick on the reef card (morning by default; evening runs to sunset). Clarity recovers about two days after strong wind; the satellite card estimates it, the satellite layer shows it. Forecasts can be wrong — check on site. Not for navigation.
